Makes a blurry drawing crisper without moving anything: merges near-identical colours into flat areas, removes speckles and strengthens light and shadow. Press it again for a stronger effect, or Undo to go back.

Changes every skin or hair tone at once and keeps the shading. Tint uses the selected colour, e.g. pick a green and tint the skin for a zombie. 🔁 Replace (top) changes any single colour everywhere.

Each size is shown at a Habit League player card (68px) and the profile (134px), the same way heads appear in the app. Press Edit to draw on that version.

Describe it (AI)

Open Claude ↗

1. Describe it and press Copy AI prompt. 2. Paste the prompt into Claude and send it. 3. Copy Claude's reply, come back and press Paste result. Starting from a drawing (an imported image, an existing head or a logo) gives much better results than starting from nothing.

Parts

Change something on Afroman (e.g. swap the moustache for a goatee), then save it as a part. Only the pixels you changed are saved, so you can add the part to any head here.

Parts kit

Build replaces the grid with a head made from these parts. Stamp adds just the hair, headband, mouth and extras on top of what you've drawn.

Start from

The image is cropped to the subject, shrunk to the grid and cleaned up. It's a starting point to tidy by hand.
